For a while I described my interest as "embodied AI" — broad enough to sound
impressive and vague enough to mean nothing. Over the past months it has
converged to a concrete question:

> **How can a robot accumulate experience through interaction, and keep adapting — to new environments, objects, and tasks — after deployment?**

Three research threads follow from that:

1. **Experience-driven learning** — memory-augmented policies and experience replay.
2. **Continual / lifelong learning** — learning new tasks without forgetting old ones.
3. **Online adaptation** — few-shot adaptation to new objects, environments, and dynamics.

VLA models, world models, and reinforcement learning are the *routes* I use
toward this, not the goal itself. I am also drawn to neuroscience-inspired
mechanisms — memory, plasticity, consolidation — as sources of better learning
algorithms.

The honest next step is to turn this into experiments I can measure.
